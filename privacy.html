Privacy Policy — Reel Dashboard

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Reel Dashboard is an internal reporting tool that shows performance numbers for the "Ha Nguyen" Facebook Page and YouTube channel to its own production team.

What data we access

With the channel owner's permission, the dashboard reads aggregate statistics for the channel's own videos (views, likes, comments, watch time, audience retention, subscribers gained, and viewer countries) through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, and similar Page statistics through the Facebook Graph API. Access is read-only.

What we do not do

We do not collect personal data from people who view the dashboard, do not use cookies for tracking, do not sell or share data with third parties, and do not post, upload, or modify anything on the channel.

Storage

Only daily aggregate totals are stored, to show day-over-day changes. Access credentials are kept as encrypted server environment variables and never sent to the browser.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
